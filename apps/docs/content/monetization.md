# Monetization (coming)

> **Coming.** How game developers make money with OGS is being decided now. This page will say how
> once it is settled. Nothing here is a commitment.

## What is true today

- **OGS costs you nothing per hour played.** The TV is drawn by the family's own devices (the host
  iPhone, the Chromecast, or a TV browser), so there's no cloud rendering bill for you or for OGS.
- **Your game is yours.** You host it, you own its domain, its players' saves and its room server.
- **Your own app is yours too.** A game shipped with [the shell](shell-config.md) is published under
  your Apple and Google accounts, with your name and listing.
- **v1 of the shell has no in-app purchases or ads.** Don't build payments into your phone page for
  the OGS app or the shell yet: store rules for web content inside an app are strict, and the way
  OGS supports purchases will decide what's allowed.

## What to do now

- Build the game. Make the first five minutes great on a real TV.
- Use [sitting labels](architecture.md#sitting-labels) and [notifications](notifications.md) well:
  whatever the model turns out to be, games people come back to will do best.
- If you have a model in mind (premium, a season pass, cosmetics, a family subscription), tell OGS:
  it helps the decision.

Cloud rendering may come back later as a separate, paid product for TVs that can't draw a game
themselves. It isn't part of OGS today, and nothing on this site depends on it.
