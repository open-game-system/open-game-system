2. TV home composition: game-night banner on its own row; full-res hero art with scrim; shelf aligned to title. No overlapping text boxes.
3. Roster/titles off the focal area within ≤2 s after a cut; nothing in centre 60%×60% after 2 s.
4. Cut-over causality across devices: phone ✓ → TV PiP stamp → kid character actually walks the path → new game expands everywhere; no frozen tail.
