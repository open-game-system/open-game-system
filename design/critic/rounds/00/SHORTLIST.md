## What every critic converged on

1. The couch swap is solved in every direction (2–3 taps, 0 QR, 0 role picks, 1-tap undo). It no longer separates them.
2. The usual ceiling is the IA: **no concept gives couch, multi-home and async one home.** Each critic's fix is the same shape: a couch layer (tonight, the TV) + a game-nights layer (households, seats, what other homes see) + one cross-game "your turn" inbox.
3. Common local gaps: iPads designed portrait (the family holds them landscape — the harness passed the brief's 820×1180 through as portrait; fixed for deep rounds), flow 9 (failures) undesigned everywhere, TV overlays that linger on the game.

## Recommendation

Take **Living Room Console** deep as the base, and fix its structural gap in pass 1 (before polish) by adding the
game-nights layer and the cross-game turn inbox above the couch, borrowing People First's game-night thread (households,
seats, "what other homes see") and Channel's cut grammar on the TV. Brand evidence favours OGS as the family's game
library/console with the household as infrastructure, and a distinctive signature still to be found (Porchlight shows the
warmer alternative).
