OGS app · spec · joining and inviting · Oct 2026

The launcher owns the door. Scan, name, you're on the couch.

Joining is a launcher feature, not a game feature. The TV shows a join QR next to its code; a guest joins from the browser with no install; every couch phone follows the TV; invites mid-game show a small QR card in the corner the game allows. Games never draw join codes. Owner-approved direction, 2026-10-05/06. Everything on this page is planned unless it says otherwise.

Design canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/5CoghVnLznn5pSdENvazJM (boards TV-Home-QR, Phone-Web-Join, Phone-Joined, TV-Game-Invite, Game-Lobby-Transfer, Sequence). Decision: ADR 2026-10-06 the launcher owns joining. Acceptance: join-and-invite.feature. Contract additions: specification.md §8.

Why

Keep our edge, steal AirConsole's door

AirConsole

Best at: scan a QR, no install, one session for many games. Weak at: phones are anonymous gamepads, ads, needs a browser on the TV.

Netflix TV games

Best at: already on every TV, polish, household profiles. Weak at: a new QR scan for every game, the Netflix app required, closed catalogue.

Rocketcrab

Best at: a party lobby that frames any web game, name passed in, a transfer link from a game's own lobby. Weak at: no TV, a spoofable host flag, no identity across nights.

Jackbox, Discord Activities

Jackbox: a room code on the TV and a web phone page, the benchmark for "no install". Discord Activities: the host's session carries everyone into a game. Neither knows who you are across games or days.

What stays ours: games know who you are (signed tokens, avatars), sittings to Continue tomorrow, kids' iPads that follow, one cast for every game. What we take: a scan-and-you're-in door, and a "play this on your TV" link from a game's own site.

1 · The join QR

A QR next to the TV code, on Home and on Getting ready

The launcher draws a card at the bottom-right of Home and Getting ready: the QR, "Scan to join, or type", the 6-character TV code, and opengame.org/join. The QR encodes https://opengame.org/join/<code>. The code still works typed in the app (the existing path). The card never covers the focal area; Home's focus row and the "3 on the couch" avatars sit on the left.

phone camera scans the QR→opengame.org/join/NT9WPH→OGS app installed: opens it, joins the couch
not installed→web join page (section 2)

Link behaviour. opengame.org/join/<code> is a universal link (iOS) and app link (Android) for the OGS app, handled as opengame://join/<code>; with no app the browser lands on the web join page. An unknown or expired code shows "That couch isn't on any more" with a field to type another code. The QR is the same code as the typed one; it adds no new credential. Where the QR is drawn: launcher only (decision 7).

Home: the phone is a remote. The app upgrade is offered at the bottom, never a gate.
  • A guest is a guest profile: name and sticker, valid for this couch session only, held in the browser (a cookie/local storage id). It is a full participant: it appears in players, gets a game token for each game, and plays on the couch.
  • The app is the upgrade, not the door. "Get the app" carries the guest's name and sticker into onboarding as the starting profile; once the app joins the same couch, the guest entry is replaced by the real profile. Profile, friends, pushes and sittings only exist in the app.
  • A guest has no @id, no friends and no push, and its sittings are not kept after the couch ends. Game tokens for a guest carry guest: true (planned; specification.md §8); sub is a per-session guest id. A game that keeps scores across nights should treat a guest as unsigned-in.
  • Guest phones play the game's startUrl in the browser itself (no WebView, no app bridge): the game's phone page sees useOgsProfile() as null and keeps its own name form unless the join page passes the guest identity (see §8 of the contract).
  • Limits: a couch accepts at most the same number of phones as today; the host can remove a phone from the couch (existing behaviour).
3 · Phones follow the TV

Every couch phone is a remote on Home and the game's controller in a game

Home: every phone shows the remote→ game starts →every couch phone opens that game's phone page→ Home →every phone is back on the remote
  • The couch session's current game drives each joined phone. A phone does not tap "Join" or pick the game; it just follows. This extends today's behaviour (the app's Playing tab pins the live game) to every phone, app or browser, and to a phone that joins mid-game: it lands straight in the running game.
  • A game swap on the TV (a different game starts) swaps every phone's page. A parked game (Home) leaves phones on the remote; Continue puts them back in the game.
  • A phone may leave the game for the remote (back) and rejoin with the return pill; the follow rule only fires when the TV's current game changes, not on every state update.
  • Same room. The phone that starts the game makes its room; every other phone opens the start page with ogsRoom=<room> once the TV page reports it (ogs:room), for every room-based game, not only multiCouch ones. Until then it stays on the remote (a plain start page would make a second, empty room). Tapping the game the TV is playing joins its room too.
  • Stepping out of the game (back) on a following phone does not park it for everyone; the host's back, or Home on any remote, does.
  • Kids' iPads follow the same way (their roster seat when one was sent, else as players), into the TV's room, and back to the remote on Home (built 2026-10-06).
  • Only the host's game page picks the TV page: a following phone's or iPad's page asking for its own TV view never reframes the TV.
  • Multi-household rooms are unchanged by this: each couch's phones follow their own TV. See the multi-couch handoff and specification.md §7.
4 · Invite mid-game

A small QR card in the corner the game allows

a phone taps Invite someoneorthe game sends ogs:invite→launcher shows the card in the manifest's safe corner
QR + "Join this game" + the TV codehides after 30 snever over the focal area
  • The card shows the same QR and TV code as Home ("Join this game", NT9WPH, "hides in 30 s"). It is drawn by the launcher over the game's frame, in the corner named by the manifest, so a game can protect its subject and HUD.
  • New optional manifest field inviteCorner: top-right (default) · top-left · bottom-right · bottom-left. The card is at most 220 × 120 CSS px at the TV's 960 × 540 reference, with a 24 px margin. A game reserves that rectangle in its layout (spec §8).
  • Triggers: any couch phone's Invite someone button (the remote, and in-game through the app's menu), or the game's ogs:invite message (for instance on its own "waiting for players" state). The launcher decides whether to show it; repeated triggers while visible restart the 30 s timer, they do not stack.
  • Joining from the card is the same as from Home: scan → join page or app → on the couch → the phone follows the TV into the running game (section 3).
  • Friend invites (push + opengame.org/play/<appId>?room=<room> for multiCouch games) are unchanged; this card is for people in the room.

Play on TV with OGS
A plain browser page on the game's own domain; the link goes to opengame.org/play/trivia-jam?room=KQTP.
  • https://opengame.org/play/<appId>?room=<room> is the same link as the friend invite (specification.md §7). Opened on a phone with the app and a cast it sends game.start { appId, room }; with no cast it asks to cast first; with no app the page says how to get it.
  • Without room it just starts the game. With room the game joins that room instead of making one, and the players already in the room are carried over by the game itself (the game owns the room).
  • The game shows the link only when it is not inside OGS (isOGSCastAvailable() is false and useOgsSession() is null). Inside OGS the game never shows it, and never shows a cast button either.

7 · Where it lives

Launcher-level, never game-level

Launcher (apps/tv)

Draws every QR and code: Home, Getting ready, the invite card. Knows the safe corner.

API + opengame.org

Resolves /join/<code> to a couch, the web join page and its guest profile; /play/<appId>.

Games

Never draw a join code, QR or room-join UI on an OGS TV. May send ogs:invite and reserve the safe corner. May keep their own room code outside OGS.

Status

Built: phones follow the TV (2026-10-06). The rest is planned

Section 3 is built for app phones and kids' iPads (couch session follows, the app opens the TV's room with ogsRoom, Home closes the game); browser guests follow once the web join page exists.
