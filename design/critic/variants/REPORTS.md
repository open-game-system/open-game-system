## v-swap-c · Instant + receipt
The swap is a cut, not a journey (~1.1 s on every device: 0.2 s save flash, 0.4 s wipe, people pop in); trust comes from what stays afterwards: a receipt chip and one-tap Back. Best: swap.02-console-menu (2×2 "Switch to" sheet; TV just dims under a Paused pill), swap.06-everyone-in (receipt chip + TV corner stamp), swap.09-ava-asleep, kid.09. Flows: swap (2 phone, 0 kid), swap-undo (1). Cost: small; needs the stream ready in < 1 s.

## v-home-c · Activity cards
Phone home = one swipeable deck of big game cards (one per open game) ordered by "needs you now": TV card first, then your turns, tonight's night, new things, paused saves, ready, waiting. Each card: art, status, who (stickers), one action; Word Duel's card is its board. Tap a card → it comes up on the TV. List view one tap away (replaces All turns). Best: home.01-phone-friday, home.11-deck-your-turn, home.12-deck-game-night, world-clock.03-inbox. Flows: home, your-turn, swap, turn-arrives, continue, tonight, deck. Cost: low (one sort over reported statuses + scroller); needs good card art per game (Tier 0 risk); one card per shot.

## v-swap-a · Shelf swap
Every couch game is a box: switching closes the lid, writes the resume point on the spine ("Mission 6 · saved 7:14 pm") and shelves it; the phone is the hand (tap a spine); each kid's character climbs out of one box into the next. Best: swap.03-saving (tv), swap.04-tv-cutover (tv + ipad), swap.02-console-menu (phone shelf of spines), swap.06-everyone-in (undo = the shelved spine). Flows: swap (2 phone, 0 kid), swap-undo (1). Cost: 1–2 days; needs box-cover art per game, fixed-length spine text, motion timing.

## v-kid-b · Window to the TV
Each kid iPad is a pane onto the TV's world: idle, it shows the TV's own game shelf over the focused game's art, the family's stickers on a window sill with the child's character centre; poking a tile lands the child's sticker on it on the iPad and the TV — a visible wish that never changes what plays. Best: kid.11-window-wish (ipad + tv), kid.12-ava-wish-paused, kid.05-ava-cutover, kid.09-juneau-bake-switch-following; asleep = curtains drawn (kid.08); unpaired = frosted glass (first-run.08). ## v-kid-a · Toy box
Each child's iPad is their own toy box: the character pops out of a painted box with their sticker; keepsakes from the games they play (planet, medal, cupcake, bunny, flower…) lie on the rug to poke, drag, jumble. Best: home.03-kid-paired-idle, kid.02-menu-paused, kid.05-ava-cutover, kid.09-juneau-bake-switch-following, first-run.08-ipads (unpainted box). Flows: swap (0 kid), first-run. ## v-kid-c · Buddy
Each child's painted character fills the iPad close-up and is the only control: poke anywhere and it giggles, hops, squishes, leans toward the finger; a small TV window sits in the corner it faces. Menu pause: the buddy turns toward the TV, lit by it. Saving: the old game gets a gold check and floats off as the buddy waves goodbye; cutover: sparkles stream from the TV; the next game lands in the buddy's hands as a glowing card a beat before it opens. Asleep: curled up in the dark. Unpaired: an empty dotted outline; on pairing the buddy pops into it. Best: home.03-kid-paired-idle, kid.02-menu-paused, kid.04-ava-saving, kid.09-juneau-bake-switch-following (+ kid.08, first-run.08). Flows: swap (0 kid), first-run. Cost: mostly CSS; per-character staging; a real wave needs posable art.

## v-night-c · Lobby table
The whole game night is one screen: a round table from above (the Hearthisle board as the tabletop, turn number in the centre, a lamp pointing at whoever rolls); each home has a seat with its crest, a status light said in words (here / on the way / away), which screen it plays on, and a lock for what it shows others. Every state is the same table (inviting, filling, declined, ready, live, paused at turn 14, a home dropped, held, finished); Nana's and Tunde's phones show it from their own chair; the TV corner shows a small version. Best: game-night.15-resumed (phone + TV), failure.40-okafors-drop (phone + TV), failure.44-nana-sees, game-night.18-finished. Flows: game-night (8 taps), game-night-resume (3), home-drop (1).
