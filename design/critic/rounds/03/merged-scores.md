## What every critic agreed on
- All four borrow a console's trade dress except the living room (Switch, Netflix/tvOS, PS5) → Visual capped ~6.5–7 unless OGS gets its own launcher language. The room is the most ownable (Visual up to 7.5) but its fixed furniture doesn't scale (20 games, 5 duels, 2 nights).
- The phone should default to touch browsing; the d-pad/touchpad remote is secondary (matches spec v3: Playing/Library are touch, the TV tab is the remote).
- Personal async turns (Word Duel) belong on the phone, not the shared TV (matches v3 tv: none).
- Motion is the weakest row everywhere: no designed cut-over. Needs tile/box → fullscreen on launch, fullscreen → tile on Home, and a synced iPad follow beat; no frozen spans.
- Overlays must stay off the game's focal area and HUD; connecting/recasting should be the launcher assembling, not a black card.
- Multi-home (invite, seats, what other homes see) is undesigned in all four.

## Recommendation
Own launcher language: the room's warmth and props (stickers on a couch, boxes with spines and resume points, warm palette) on a scalable, status-first layout (Continue · Tonight · Library), with the box↔fullscreen cut-over as the signature motion. Phone per spec v3.
